    ⌄ 💡 Problem Statement, Our AI Solution & How It Works
    ⚠️ The Local Problem (Hamara Masla)In schools and towns (Tarai, Golarchi, Badin), recyclable Plastic & Paper get mixed in open dumps with zero daily waste count data and no fast way to alert Union Council / Municipal officers.
    💡 Our Integrated AI Solution (Hamara Hal)A low-cost Edge AI Camera + 2-Bin See-Saw Smart Bin (35° Left Sky Blue / 145° Right Pink) that auto-sorts waste, records a live Google Cloud Waste Census, and sends geotagged Civic Alerts.
    1️⃣ Open AI Camera➔2️⃣ Scan Plastic / Paper➔3️⃣ See-Saw Bin Sorts➔4️⃣ Cloud Census & Civic Action

    ⌄ 🌟 Specifications, Highlights & Testing Summary
    1. Integrated 4-in-1 Local SolutionCombines Edge AI Vision + See-Saw IoT Smart Bin + Live Google Sheet Waste Census + Halqa Civic Portal.
    2. Reliable See-Saw SortingUses a center-pivoted See-Saw flap (35° Left for Plastic/Sky Blue Bin, 145° Right for Paper/Pink Bin) so items slide smoothly.
    3. Safe 5V Design & Clean BuildPowered by safe 5V laptop USB, protected by 220-Ohm resistors, with color-coded Sky Blue & Pink compartments.
    4. Recycled & Low-Cost MaterialsBuilt using reused cardboard cartons, wooden sticks, and free open-source web tools.
    ⌄ 📖 Easy Technical Glossary (Asaan Lafzon Mein)
    🧠 Edge AI (TensorFlow.js): AI model jo internet server ke bajaye seedha browser ke andar chalta hai.
    ⚙️ See-Saw Digital Twin: Screen par bana Smart Bin jo asli Arduino Servo (35° Sky Blue / 145° Pink) ki tarah jhuk kar dikhata hai.
    📊 Waste Census: Kachre ki rozana ginti jo khud-ba-khud Google Sheet mein jama hoti hai.
    🔌 Web Serial IoT: USB cable se seedha browser ko Arduino Uno ke sath jorne wali technology.
