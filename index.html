♻️ About Saaf Sindh AI (Theme 2)

Saaf Sindh AI ek 4-in-1 Civic & Edge-AI Automation system hai jo GBHSS Haji Junejo Tarai (SEMIS: 401020169) ke students Shameer Ali, Zahid Ali, aur Farhan ne Guide Mentor Ghulam Hussain Memon ki rahnumai mein banaya hai.

  • 1. Civic Portal: UC Tarai, TC Golarchi aur MC Badin ke liye geotagged photo complaint aur live Report ID tracking.
  • 2. Smart Waste Census: Google Sheet (Waste_Audit) mein Plastic aur Paper ka live digital record.
  • 3. AI + Arduino See-Saw Bin: Teachable Machine (RP91qAZCY) + SG90 Servo See-Saw (35° Plastic / 145° Paper).
  • 4. 60s Gamification: Bachon ke liye 60-second AI Recycling Challenge.
