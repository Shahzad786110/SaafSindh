    ⌄ 💡 Problem Statement, Our AI Solution & How It Works
    ⚠️ The Local Problem (Hamara Masla) In schools and towns (Tarai, Golarchi, Badin), recyclable Plastic & Paper get mixed in open dumps with zero daily waste count data and no fast way to alert Union Council / Municipal officers.
    💡 Our Integrated AI Solution (Hamara Hal) A low-cost Edge AI Camera + 2-Bin See-Saw Smart Bin (35° Left / 145° Right) that auto-sorts waste, records a live Google Cloud Waste Census, and sends geotagged Civic Alerts to Halqa Officers.
    1️⃣ Open AI Camera ➔ 2️⃣ Scan Plastic / Paper ➔ 3️⃣ See-Saw Bin Sorts ➔ 4️⃣ Cloud Census & Civic Action
    ⌄ 👥 Student Team Roles (Shameer Ali • Zahid Ali • Farhan)
    🚨 Civic & Security Lead Shameer Ali Mapped local cleanliness workflows for UC Tarai, TC Golarchi & MC Badin, testing GPS photo reporting, role-based Halqa Officer access control & recycled cardboard See-Saw bin structure.
    🤖 AI & Hardware Lead Zahid Ali Trained the Teachable Machine vision model (Plastic, Paper & Empty classes) and assembled the 2-bin See-Saw Flap with Arduino Uno & SG90 Servo motor.
    📊 Data & Census Lead Farhan Designed the Google Sheets Day-Wise Waste Census, recorded accuracy testing log, and 60-second student challenge game.
    ⌄ 🌟 Specifications, Highlights & Testing Summary
    1. Integrated 4-in-1 Local Solution Combines Edge AI Vision + See-Saw IoT Smart Bin + Live Google Sheet Waste Census + Halqa Civic Portal for Tarai, Golarchi & Badin.
    2. Reliable See-Saw Sorting Uses a center-pivoted See-Saw flap (`35°` Left for Plastic/Blue Bin, `145°` Right for Paper/Green Bin) so items slide smoothly by gravity.
    3. Safe 5V Design & Clean Build Powered by safe 5V laptop USB (zero 220V AC mains hazard), protected by 220-Ohm resistors, with color-coded Blue & Green compartments.
    4. Recycled & Low-Cost Materials Built using reused cardboard cartons, wooden sticks, and free open-source web tools to keep the prototype affordable for any school.
    ⌄ 📖 Easy Technical Glossary (Asaan Lafzon Mein)
    🧠 Edge AI (TensorFlow.js): AI model jo internet server ke bajaye seedha aapke mobile/laptop ke browser ke andar chalta hai—tez aur safe!
    ⚙️ See-Saw Digital Twin: Screen par bana hua Smart Bin jo bilkul asli Arduino Servo motor (35° Left / 145° Right) ki tarah live jhuk kar dikhata hai.
    📊 Waste Census: Kachre ki rozana ginti (Plastic aur Paper numbers) jo khud-ba-khud Google Sheet mein jama hoti hai.
    🔌 Web Serial IoT: Bina mehnge Wi-Fi module ke seedha USB cable se browser ko Arduino Uno ke sath jorne wali modern technology.
