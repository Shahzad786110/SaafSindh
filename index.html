♻️ About Saaf Sindh AI (Theme 2: AI, Automation & Digital Solution)

Saaf Sindh AI ek 4-in-1 Civic & Edge-AI Automation system hai jo GBHSS Haji Junejo Tarai (SEMIS: 401020169) ke students Shameer Ali, Zahid Ali, aur Farhan ne Mentor Ghulam Hussain Memon (BCS — University of Sindh, Laar Campus Badin) ki rahnumai mein tayyar kiya hai.

  • 1. Civic Report Portal: Union Council (UC) Tarai, Town Committee (TC) Golarchi aur Municipal Committee (MC) Badin ke liye geotagged photo complaint aur live Report ID tracking.
  • 2. Smart Waste Census: Google Sheet (Waste_Audit) mein Plastic aur Paper ka day-wise live digital hisab, graph aur AI insights.
  • 3. AI + Arduino See-Saw Smart Bin: Google Teachable Machine (RP91qAZCY) + Arduino Uno & SG90 Servo See-Saw (35° Left Plastic / 145° Right Paper).
  • 4. 60s Gamification Challenge: Students mein safai aur recycling ka shauq paida karne ke liye 60-second AI Target Challenge.
